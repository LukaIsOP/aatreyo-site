Hi, my name is Aatreyo and I'm a student at Berkeley. I built this website to be an evolving record of my ideas, the reasoning behind them, and the mistakes I learn from along the way.

Probability was the first subject that changed how I think about markets. A fair coin can land heads five times in a row, and a good bet can lose while a bad one wins. A single outcome says very little about whether the thinking behind it was sound. What matters is whether the odds were on your side when you made the call, and whether you were honest about what you didn't know.

Here I'll share the companies I've pitched, what's capturing my curiosity, and how each idea plays out, good or bad. I hope it becomes an honest record of how I grow as an investor.
